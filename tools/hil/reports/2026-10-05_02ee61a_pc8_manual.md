# Manual PWM check: PC8 (thruster 7), firmware 02ee61a

2026-10-05. Closes the one PWM pin the scripted sweep (run `d53de2b`) did not
measure: the jumper had landed on the neighbouring pin. Same method as
`hil_rtos.py`: PA15 (TIM2_CH1 input capture) jumpered to PC8 (CN10 pin 2), bench
keys sent from a serial terminal on the ST-LINK VCP at 115200. `P` toggles the
per-channel signature (channel k at 1,100 + 100k us, so channel 6 = 1,700 us),
`p` prints and clears the capture window. The first report after each change
spans the change and is not counted.

Console output, transcribed from the terminal:

```text
B:pwm signature=0
B:pwm n=275 hi=1500-1700 per=20000-20000 sig=0
B:pwm n=8 hi=1500-1500 per=20000-20000 sig=0
B:pwm n=10 hi=1500-1500 per=20000-20000 sig=0
B:pwm n=7 hi=1500-1500 per=20000-20000 sig=0
B:pwm n=9 hi=1500-1500 per=20000-20000 sig=0
B:pwm signature=1
B:pwm n=532 hi=1500-1700 per=20000-20000 sig=1
B:pwm n=10 hi=1700-1700 per=20000-20000 sig=1
B:pwm n=7 hi=1700-1700 per=20000-20000 sig=1
B:pwm n=35 hi=1700-1700 per=20000-20000 sig=1
```

Result: PC8 carries channel 6 exactly (1,700 us, 52 pulses across three
windows) with a 20,000 us period, and returns to 1,500 us neutral with the
signature off (34 pulses across four windows).
